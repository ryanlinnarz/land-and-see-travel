MEET TRUDY

What is Trudy Travels?

Follow Trudy and her bunny through a day at the beach, with story scenes to color and activities to explore together.

Inside are 10 beach-themed coloring pages, 10 simple activities, a certificate, and space for notes and doodles. Kids can pack a beach bag on paper, try a beach maze, draw seashells, and imagine their own perfect beach day.

COLOR · NOTICE · TALK

What kids do and learn.

Coloring, observation, and imagination come together:

  • Get creative. Color Trudy and Bunny’s beach-day scenes.
  • Notice the journey. Look for pictures in Road Trip I-Spy and help find Bunny.
  • Imagine your own adventure. Draw seashells, a new hiding place for Bunny, and your perfect beach day.

TRAVELING TOGETHER

Who it’s for.

Parents, grandparents, and other grown-ups looking for a travel-themed coloring activity to share with kids.

Use the sample pages to decide whether the book fits your child’s interests and coloring ability.

For ages 3–9, with a grown-up’s help as needed.
